ListTube Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

ListTube works without requiring an account. Watchlists, ratings, progress, preferences, and offline media metadata are stored locally on your device in IndexedDB and localStorage.

Optional Cloud Sync & Third-Party Trackers

If you choose to sign in with Google, AniList, or MyAnimeList, ListTube stores your profile metadata, watchlist entries, likes, comments, reviews, and connected tracker preferences in our managed PostgreSQL backend solely to synchronize your library across devices and power optional social features.

Public Visibility Controls

You can hide your watch history, statistics, favorites, or public watchlist at any time inside Settings → Privacy & Alerts.

External Links & Media APIs

ListTube queries public anime/manga metadata APIs (AniList, Jikan/MyAnimeList, iTunes audio previews, and Wikipedia) to display series information. Opening external watch or read links takes you to third-party sites governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Deletion & Contact

You may clear local storage/cache at any time in Settings → Clean & Refresh Cache or request cloud account deletion by emailing the repository maintainer via GitHub Issues.
